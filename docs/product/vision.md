# SmartPoints Product Vision

## Status

Discovery required — no product decisions have been approved.

## Working premise

SmartPoints will help kids earth points for completing tasks, learning new skills, and demonstrating good behavior. Parents will be able to set up tasks and rewards, track progress, and manage the points system.

This will be a web app, basic user authentication, and a simple points system. The initial MVP will focus on the core functionality of task management, point tracking, and reward redemption.
The main idea is the make point earning fun and engaging for kids, while giving parents a simple way to manage and monitor their children's progress. It should be easy to mark completed tasks (on click -> icon). And also to mark rewards that have been redeemed. The app should provide a clear overview of the points earned, tasks completed, and rewards redeemed.

The Ux should be simple and intuitive, with a focus on gamification elements to keep kids motivated. The app should also provide parents with insights into their children's progress and behavior (dashboard view). The app should be designed to be safe and secure for children, with appropriate privacy controls and parental oversight. 

## To decide before implementation

- Target user and the specific problem to solve.
- Which kinds of points or rewards are in scope.
- The first user-visible MVP outcome.
- Data sources, privacy expectations, and whether users connect external accounts.
- Monetization, provider, and AI boundaries.

## Non-MVP until explicitly approved

- External account connections or imported personal financial data.
- Paid providers, payments, or subscription billing.
- Automated actions that change a user's account or financial position.
- AI features that process personal data.
